How To Play Word Hunt

1. Connect Adjacent Letters: Drag or swipe across adjacent letters in any direction—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally—to spell words.

2. Word Minimum: Words must be at least 3 letters long. You cannot use the exact same grid tile twice in a single word.

3. Speed Challenge: Find as many words as possible. Under **Classic Blitz** you have exactly 80 seconds. Under **Zen Mode**, you take your time to build the perfect dictionary!
